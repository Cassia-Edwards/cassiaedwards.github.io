### About me

I am a second year PhD student at the University of Edinburgh as part of the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Algebra, Geometry and Quantum Fields <a href= "https://www.agq-cdt.org">(AGQ CDT) </a> under the supervision of <a href ="https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~cbarwick/"> Dr. Clark Barwick </a>. 

I am interested in broadly interested in homotopy theory and infinity-categories. In particular, I am interested in factorisation algebras. See the Quick Links section for things that I am currently involved in organising!

Outside of maths, I make music, do various crafts, climb and play various generes of videogames with varying degrees of skill.

I am a <a href="https://martingale.foundation"> Martingale Scholar</a>.
